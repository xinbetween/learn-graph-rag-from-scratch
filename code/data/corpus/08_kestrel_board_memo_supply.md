# CONFIDENTIAL: Memo to the Board of Directors

**From:** Dr. Mira Okafor, CEO, Kestrel Labs
**To:** Kestrel Labs Board of Directors (incl. Lena Park, Brightwater Capital)
**Date:** 14 May 2025
**Subject:** Sonar supply disruption and proposed response

## 1. What happened

On 2 May we received formal notice that Deepcast will not renew our supply agreement and will stop accepting new orders from outside customers at the end of June. Deepcast has supplied every sonar module in our fleet since 2022. The notice follows Deepcast's change of ownership last month; the new owner intends to reserve its production for internal programs.

We will receive the units already on order, but after June we have no source for new sonar modules.

## 2. Why it matters

Every Sentinel drone depends on these modules. Our current inventory covers roughly nine months of normal operations, including replacements for units lost or damaged at sea. That means:

- Project Sentinel operations on Halden Reef are secure through early 2026, but not beyond.
- We cannot grow the Sentinel fleet for new customers without a new sonar source.
- Our pipeline of monitoring contracts, several of which depend on upcoming regulation, is at risk if we cannot promise delivery.

I want to be direct: this is the most serious operational risk Kestrel Labs has faced. Relying on a single supplier was a reasonable choice in 2022. It is not a reasonable choice now.

## 3. Options considered

**A. Switch to another vendor.** Two alternatives exist. Both have lead times above twelve months and neither matches the size and power budget of our drones. We would need to redesign the hull.

**B. Build our own sonar module.** Our acoustic array design is already in-house; what we bought from Deepcast was the transducers and signal-processing boards. Jonas Vehl estimates that a small dedicated team could produce a working in-house module within twelve to fifteen months.

**C. Stockpile and wait.** Buy remaining units at any price and hope the market changes. This only delays the problem.

## 4. Recommendation

Management recommends Option B, with Option A pursued in parallel as a fallback. We propose to start a dedicated internal engineering project in June, co-led by Jonas Vehl (CTO) and Priya Nair (lead engineer, Project Sentinel), with a goal of replacing every Deepcast unit in the fleet by the end of 2026.

The estimated cost is $3.5 million over eighteen months. This is not covered by our current runway and will need to be part of the funding round we are preparing for later this year.

## 5. Decision requested

We ask the board to approve the in-house sonar program and the associated budget at the meeting on 21 May. Lena Park has asked for a supplier risk review to be included in the agenda, and we will circulate it in advance.

Mira Okafor

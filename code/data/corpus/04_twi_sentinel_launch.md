# Launching Project Sentinel: Eyes on Halden Reef All Year Round

*Tidewater Institute News blog. Posted 21 February 2023.*

Tidewater Institute is delighted to announce Project Sentinel, a new reef-monitoring program run by Kestrel Labs with Tidewater Institute as its research partner. Starting this spring, a small fleet of autonomous drones will patrol Halden Reef, the coral reef about 40 km off Port Avalon, collecting measurements that until now we could only gather a few days each season.

Halden Reef has been studied by TWI scientists for more than thirty years. It is one of the healthiest reefs in the region and also one of the most closely watched, because changes there often signal what will happen to smaller reefs nearer the coast. Dr. Ana Ruiz, Director of Tidewater Institute, is the principal investigator of the long-running Halden Reef survey and will lead the scientific side of the new program.

"For three decades our picture of Halden Reef has been a series of snapshots," said Dr. Ana Ruiz. "Project Sentinel turns those snapshots into a film. The drones will stay on the reef through storms, through winter, through the nights when nobody from TWI could possibly be out there."

## How the partnership works

Kestrel Labs runs Project Sentinel: the company builds, deploys and maintains the Sentinel drones, and its engineers operate the fleet from Port Avalon. Tidewater Institute designs the sampling plan, calibrates the water-quality sensors and analyses the data as part of the Halden Reef survey. All data collected by Sentinel will be published through the institute's open reef data portal after quality control.

Each Sentinel drone carries a temperature and light logger, a chemistry package and an acoustic array. The sonar array layout was designed by Kestrel Labs co-founder Jonas Vehl, and lets the drones map the three-dimensional structure of the reef and count fish schools without disturbing them.

Many readers will remember that Kestrel Labs chief executive Dr. Mira Okafor spent six years as a researcher here before founding the company. "It is a special feeling to bring this back to Tidewater," Okafor said at the launch event. "Ana Ruiz and her team know Halden Reef better than anyone alive. Our job is to give them the tools."

## What comes next

The first four drones will be deployed in April 2023, with the fleet growing to twelve by the end of the year. Monthly updates from Project Sentinel will appear on this blog, and TWI will host an open day in Port Avalon in the summer where visitors can see the drones up close.

Tidewater Institute is an independent marine research institute based in Port Avalon. It studies reef ecology, coastal water quality and fisheries, and trains early-career marine scientists from across the region.

# Port Avalon City Council: Minutes of Regular Meeting

*Council Chamber, Port Avalon City Hall. 18 November 2025, 6:30 pm.*

**Present:** Mayor and eight councillors. **Staff:** City Manager, Director of Environmental Services, City Attorney.
**Guests:** Dr. Ana Ruiz (TWI); Dr. Mira Okafor (Kestrel Labs).

## 1. Call to order and approval of minutes

The Mayor called the meeting to order at 6:32 pm. Minutes of the 4 November meeting were approved without amendment.

## 2. Public comment

Two residents spoke in support of item 5. One resident asked whether drone monitoring would disturb recreational divers; staff agreed to respond in writing.

## 3. Harbour dredging schedule

Deferred to the December meeting at the request of staff.

## 4. Library renovation budget

Approved, 9 to 0.

## 5. Ocean Health Act compliance: reef monitoring services contract

The Director of Environmental Services presented the staff report. Under the Ocean Health Act, the city must submit quarterly reef monitoring reports for the reefs within its coastal zone, beginning in the first quarter of 2026. Staff estimated that meeting the requirement with dive teams alone would cost the city roughly three times the proposed contract and would still not provide continuous coverage.

Staff recommended a three-year monitoring services contract with Kestrel Labs, a Port Avalon company, using its Sentinel drones. Staff noted that Sentinel is already operating on Halden Reef and that the vendor would extend coverage to the two nearshore reefs managed by the city.

Dr. Ana Ruiz, Director of Tidewater Institute, spoke in support. She stated that TWI has analysed Sentinel data since 2023 and that the drones gave nine days of early warning before the 2024 bleaching event. She recommended that the contract require open publication of the data, and that TWI continue to provide independent scientific review.

Councillor Adeyemi asked about the vendor's reliability given press reports of a sonar supply problem. Dr. Okafor responded that the company holds enough spare modules to cover the contract's first year and that its in-house replacement module is in harbour testing.

Councillor Brandt asked whether the contract created a conflict of interest, since the vendor's CEO was formerly employed by TWI. The City Attorney advised that no conflict exists because the institute is not a party to the contract and receives no payment under it.

**Motion:** To authorise the City Manager to execute a three-year reef monitoring services contract with Kestrel Labs for Ocean Health Act compliance, with a condition that monitoring data be published openly and reviewed by Tidewater Institute.
**Moved:** Councillor Adeyemi. **Seconded:** Councillor Brandt.
**Vote:** Carried, 8 to 1. The Port Avalon City Council thereby contracted Kestrel Labs to provide Ocean Health Act compliance monitoring.

# "We Stopped Being a Startup the Day Deepcast Said No": An Interview With Kestrel Labs Leadership

*Ocean Tech Podcast, episode 112, transcript excerpt. Recorded 26 March 2026.*

**HOST:** Big news this month. Kestrel Labs announced that Priya Nair is the company's new chief technology officer, and co-founder Jonas Vehl is moving into a new role. Mira, Priya, Jonas, thanks for joining.

**OKAFOR:** Thanks for having us. Yes, as of March 2026 Priya is our CTO, and Jonas has become Chief Scientist.

**HOST:** Jonas, you were CTO for five years. Why step down now?

**VEHL:** Because the job changed. When Mira and I started this company in 2021, being CTO meant soldering boards and designing the sonar array myself. Now it means running a sixty-person engineering organisation with production lines and customer contracts. Priya is far better at that than I am. As Chief Scientist I get to go back to acoustics research, which is what I love.

**HOST:** Priya, you joined in 2023 from Marlow Dynamics. Now you're CTO. What's the priority?

**NAIR:** Finishing Tern. Project Tern is in sea trials on two Sentinel drones right now, and the results are good. By the end of this year every drone should be running our own sonar instead of Deepcast hardware. After that it's scale: the Port Avalon City Council contract with Kestrel Labs starts this quarter and other municipalities are following.

**HOST:** Mira, you've called 2025 the hardest year for the company. What are the risks now?

**OKAFOR:** A few. First, execution risk on Tern. If the fleet replacement slips, we are running on our spare Deepcast modules, and those will not last forever. Second, competition. Marlow Dynamics bought Deepcast last year, and now Marlow is marketing smaller vehicles to exactly the civilian customers we serve. They are a serious competitor with far more money than us. Third, we depend heavily on the Ocean Health Act. It created most of our new demand, and if the regulation is weakened or delayed, our pipeline shrinks. And fourth, concentration: most of our data and reputation comes from one reef, Halden Reef, and one scientific partner.

**HOST:** You mean Tidewater?

**OKAFOR:** Yes. TWI has been extraordinary. Ana Ruiz and her team turned our drones into science. But we need to prove Sentinel works on other reefs with other partners.

**HOST:** Jonas, any regrets about buying sonar from Deepcast back in 2022?

**VEHL:** No. It was the right call for a ten-person company. The mistake would have been not having a plan B. We learned that the hard way.

**HOST:** Last question. What does the board think of the leadership change?

**OKAFOR:** Lena Park and the rest of the board supported it unanimously. Brightwater has been with us since the Series A, and they pushed us to build a leadership team that could run a real company. This is part of that.

**HOST:** Mira Okafor, Priya Nair and Jonas Vehl of Kestrel Labs, thank you.

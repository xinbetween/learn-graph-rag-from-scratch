# Kestrel Labs Raises $40 Million Series B as Reef Monitoring Becomes Law

*Press release. Port Avalon, 23 September 2025.*

Kestrel Labs today announced a $40 million Series B financing led by Brightwater Capital, which also led the company's Series A in 2022. Existing investors participated alongside two new infrastructure funds. The round brings total funding raised by Kestrel Labs to $52 million.

The company will use the funds to scale production of its autonomous monitoring drones, complete development of its in-house sonar module, and expand operations beyond Halden Reef to additional reef sites along the coast.

## A new market created by regulation

The financing comes two months after the regional assembly passed the Ocean Health Act in July 2025. The Ocean Health Act requires coastal municipalities and operators of marine infrastructure to carry out quarterly reef monitoring, using methods that can detect bleaching, sedimentation and water-quality changes. Compliance begins in 2026.

Supporters of the Act cited the 2024 Halden Reef bleaching event, which was detected by autonomous drones days before divers saw it, as evidence that continuous monitoring is both possible and affordable.

"A year ago, reef monitoring was something scientists hoped for. Today the Ocean Health Act makes it a legal obligation," said Lena Park, partner at Brightwater Capital and a member of the Kestrel Labs board. "Project Sentinel has already proven it can do the job on one of the most demanding reefs in the region. We are doubling down because the demand is now real, and because this team handled a very difficult year with discipline."

## Growth and resilience

Kestrel Labs chief executive Okafor pointed to the company's response to the loss of its sonar supplier earlier this year as a turning point.

"Spring 2025 taught us that we cannot depend on a single outside supplier for the most important part of our drones," Okafor said. "Project Tern, our in-house sonar program, is the answer to that, and this round funds it through to fleet deployment. We will come out of this more vertically integrated and more resilient."

Kestrel Labs now employs 64 people in Port Avalon and operates a fleet of 18 Sentinel drones. The company says it is in discussions with several municipalities about Ocean Health Act compliance contracts.

## About Brightwater Capital

Brightwater Capital is a venture firm investing in climate resilience and ocean technology. Lena Park leads its ocean practice and has sat on the Kestrel Labs board since 2022.

## About Kestrel Labs

Kestrel Labs, founded in 2021 by Dr. Mira Okafor and Jonas Vehl, builds autonomous underwater drones for continuous reef and coastal monitoring. Through Project Sentinel, run in partnership with Tidewater Institute, Kestrel drones monitor Halden Reef around the clock.

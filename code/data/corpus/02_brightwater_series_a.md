# Brightwater Capital Leads $12 Million Series A in Kestrel Labs

*Press release. Port Avalon, 15 March 2022.*

Kestrel Labs, the Port Avalon developer of autonomous underwater drones for environmental monitoring, today announced that it has raised $12 million in Series A funding. The round was led by Brightwater Capital, a venture firm that invests in climate and ocean technology, with participation from two regional angel groups.

The new capital will be used to expand the engineering team, move the company from its prototype stage to a small production line, and fund long-duration sea trials of the first Kestrel Labs drone fleet. The company expects to grow from twelve to thirty employees over the next eighteen months.

As part of the financing, Lena Park, a partner at Brightwater Capital, will join the Kestrel Labs board of directors. Lena Park leads Brightwater Capital's ocean technology practice and has previously served on the boards of two companies working on aquaculture sensors and offshore wind inspection.

"Kestrel Labs is building the kind of infrastructure the ocean has always lacked: persistent, affordable observation," said Lena Park. "Dr. Mira Okafor and Jonas Vehl combine deep scientific credibility with real hardware experience. We believe reef and coastal monitoring will become a requirement, not a nice-to-have, and we want to back the team that is ready when it does."

Dr. Mira Okafor, co-founder and CEO of Kestrel Labs, said the partnership was about more than money. "Brightwater understands that hardware takes patience. Having Lena Park on our board gives us someone who has watched ocean companies scale and knows where they stumble."

## About the round

- Amount: $12 million
- Stage: Series A
- Lead investor: Brightwater Capital
- New board member: Lena Park, Partner, Brightwater Capital

Brightwater Capital was founded in 2014 and manages funds focused on climate resilience. Its portfolio includes companies in grid storage, water treatment and marine sensing. The firm typically takes a board seat in companies where it leads a round.

## About Kestrel Labs

Kestrel Labs was founded in 2021 in Port Avalon by Dr. Mira Okafor and Jonas Vehl. The company designs autonomous underwater drones that can remain on station for weeks, collecting acoustic, optical and chemical measurements of reefs and coastal waters. Kestrel Labs works closely with marine scientists and plans to announce its first research partnership later this year.

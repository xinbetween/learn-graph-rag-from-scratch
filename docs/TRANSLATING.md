# Translating into Chinese

The Chinese site mirrors the English one path for path under `site/zh/`. Translation is partial by design:
a chapter that has not been translated shows its English body under a banner, while navigation, search,
quizzes and figure controls are already in Chinese. `/zh/` is complete and navigable at every stage.

## How the mirror works

- `site/zh/index.html` is written by hand.
- `site/zh/chapters/<slug>.html` is generated by `tools/build_zh.py` from the English page, with
  `data-fallback="en"` on `<body>`, until someone translates it.
- A translated page carries `data-translated="true"` on `<body>` (and no `data-fallback`). `build_zh.py` then
  leaves its content alone and only normalizes the head and script tags.
- Chrome strings live in `site/assets/js/site.js` (`STRINGS.zh`) and `site/assets/js/viz.js` (`L`, the
  figure controls and built-in captions). Chapter titles, summaries and part text live in
  `site/assets/js/curriculum.zh.js`.

## Translating a chapter

1. Start from the generated fallback: `site/zh/chapters/<slug>.html` already has the right head, asset
   paths and scripts. Replace `data-fallback="en"` with `data-translated="true"` on `<body>`.
2. Translate everything a reader sees inside `<main>`: headings, prose, lists, tables, captions, callout
   titles, quiz questions, options and explanations, exercise text and worked answers, Q&A, project
   checklists, key takeaways, the `<title>` and `<meta name="description">`, SVG `<text>` and `<title>`
   elements, and the `title`, `subtitle`, `caption`, `detail`, `note`, `intro`, `question`, `final` and
   `answer` strings inside figure JSON. Code comments and printed explanations inside worked answers may be
   translated; code itself may not.
3. Keep the shape identical. `tools/check_site.py` compares every translated page with its English source
   and fails if any of these differ: the sequence of figure types, the number of diagrams, exercises,
   answers, Q&A items, projects, key-takeaway blocks and references, and which option is correct in every
   quiz. Do not reorder quiz options.
4. Run `python3 tools/build_zh.py && python3 tools/stamp_assets.py && python3 tools/check_site.py`, then
   render-check the page (`python3 tools/serve.py` in the background, `python3 tools/render_check.py`).

## What stays in English

- Code, identifiers, CLI commands, file paths, config keys, JSON keys, Cypher and SPARQL.
- Names of models, libraries, frameworks, datasets, benchmarks and companies: GraphRAG, LightRAG, HippoRAG,
  Neo4j, NetworkX, HotpotQA, Microsoft Research.
- Paper titles in references and citations, and author names.
- The Kestrel Labs world: Kestrel Labs, Project Tern, Project Sentinel, Deepcast, Marlow Dynamics, Tidewater
  Institute, Brightwater Capital, Halden Reef, Port Avalon, the Ocean Health Act, and every person's name.
  Uppercase relation types (`CTO_OF`, `SUPPLIED`) stay as they are; lowercase edge labels that are ordinary
  words (acquired, supplied) are translated (收购, 供货).
- Figure node `id` values and edge references (`"a>b"`). Node `label`s that are entity names stay English;
  labels that are ordinary words may be translated.
- Exact quotations from the corpus documents, which are English sources. Quote them in English and add a
  Chinese gloss where the meaning matters.

## Style

- Write natural, professional simplified Chinese, as a Chinese technical author would, not a sentence-by-
  sentence rendering of the English. Restructure long English sentences; split where Chinese reads better.
- Keep the technical content exact. Numbers, formulas, units, ranges and hedges (“about”, “roughly”,
  “in our run”) must survive translation unchanged. Do not add claims or drop caveats.
- Write large numbers the Chinese way (739 万 token, 1200 万美元); keep prices with `$` as in the source.
- Use full-width Chinese punctuation in Chinese text (，。：；？！“”（）), and ASCII punctuation inside code.
- Put a half-width space between Chinese characters and adjacent Latin words or numbers: 使用 PageRank 时，
  取 α = 0.85。 No space next to full-width punctuation.
- Use 你 to address the reader, as the English uses “you”.
- Headings in plain declarative form, no trailing punctuation. The component headings are fixed:
  你将学会 (objectives), 练习, 检验理解, 常见问题, 本章要点, 参考文献; exercise levels 热身 / 核心 / 进阶;
  answer summaries 查看解答 and 提示.
- Avoid translationese: 被 overuse, 进行 + verb (进行检索 → 检索), 的 chains, 一个 before every noun, and
  literal renderings of English idioms.
